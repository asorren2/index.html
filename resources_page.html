    Remote Work and Mental Health

    This data set is from Kaggle and contains the following 10 columns: Employee ID, Age, Gender, Job Role, Industry, Work Location, Stress Level, Mental Health Condition, Social Isolation Rating, Satisfaction with Remote Work

    Anxiety and Depression Mental Health Factors

    This data set is from Kaggle and contains the following 21 columns: Age, Gender, Education Level, Employment Status, Sleep Hours, Physical Activity Hrs, Social Support Score, Anxiety Score, Depression Score, Stress Level, Family History Mental Illness, Chronic Illnesses, Medication Use, Therapy, Meditation, Substance Use, Financial Stress, Work Stress, Self Esteem Score, Life Satisfaction Score, Loneliness Score

    Student Lifestyle Dataset
